—— about

Turning system complexity
into human clarity

Multidisciplinary Experience Designer with 18+ years shipping across field operations, industrial software, enterprise platforms, and healthcare products — from startups to Fortune 500 companies.

I prototype in code using Claude and coding agents, which means stakeholders see a working interface, not a static mockup, from the first review. Research-grounded, systems-minded — I like taking a complex legacy system apart and turning it into something people can actually use, in ways that show up in the numbers.

Based in Chiapas, MX. Recognized with the 2020 AT&T International Accessibility in Action Award for inclusive design at national scale. Currently at SoftServe spanning healthcare, construction SaaS, analytics, and emerging tech (XR / Metaverse).

IA & Design Systems Accessibility Product Design AI-Native Prototyping

Design & prototyping: Figma, Affinity, Sketch, Adobe CC, AEM, Penpot. AI agents & dev: Claude, Cursor, local LLMs. Research: Miro, Maze, UserTesting, HotJar.
