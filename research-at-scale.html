How Might We
// hmw
make rigorous UX research accessible and actionable for designers and PMs who don't have a research background, across multiple product verticals at once?
The Problem

Research was siloed inside the research department. Designers and PMs either waited weeks for research outputs or made decisions without them. The bottleneck wasn't data collection. It was the gap between sessions and actionable insight.

AI tools were available across the organization but unused in any structured research workflow. There were no standardized guidelines enabling designers or PMs to self-serve research at a quality bar the team could trust.

Visual Context

Generic research-process template board

Actual usability-testing session recordings, transcripts, and participant feedback are client-confidential and withheld.

The Solution

Built research guidelines enabling designers and PMs to self-serve qualitative research without a research background. They cover study design, recruitment via Ethnio, session facilitation with UserTesting.com, and synthesis standards.

Introduced AI-assisted synthesis to close the gap between raw sessions and insight delivery. Established cross-functional feedback loops so research findings moved into product decisions in days, not weeks. Deployed across multiple verticals simultaneously.

Key Decision
// design-decision

I introduced AI-assisted synthesis because the bottleneck wasn't data collection. It was the time between sessions and actionable insight. AI cut that gap without reducing quality, as long as it was paired with human judgment at the interpretation layer. Speed without shortcuts.

// reflection

The best outcome wasn't the speed improvement. It was the cultural shift. When designers started asking 'what does the research say?' before writing a user story, the work had done what it was meant to do. Process adoption is the real design deliverable.
